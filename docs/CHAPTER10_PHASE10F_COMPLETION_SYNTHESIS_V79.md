# Chapter 10 Phase 10F / V79 — Research Completion Gate & Analyst Growth Synthesis

Source lock remains **Michael Shearn — The Investment Checklist — Chapter 10 — Q53–Q57**, printed pages 281–303. Chapter 11 starts at Q58 and is excluded.

## Purpose

V79 adds a deterministic research-completion gate and a neutral report/synthesis contract for Chapter 10. The gate answers only whether the required research fields have been resolved sufficiently for analyst review. It does **not** judge growth quality.

A question is research-ready only when: research status is `Answered` or `N/A`; confidence is no longer `Unknown`; analyst assessment is present; and every source-locked evidence dimension for that question has a non-Unknown status. Chapter readiness requires all Q53–Q57 to be research-ready.

## Analyst-owned synthesis

The growth synthesis is entirely analyst-authored. It contains status plus takeaways for growth route, profitability, runway, pace/funding, strengths, concerns, unknowns, final growth synthesis and analyst note. No field is populated automatically from evidence direction or completion state.

## Investment boundary

Research completion is not a Growth Score, weighted score, growth forecast, intrinsic-value calculation, valuation conclusion, MOS change, Investment Research Gate change or BUY/HOLD/SELL signal. Canonical financial data remains in existing SSOTs; V79 creates no financial engine and no duplicate CCC calculation.
