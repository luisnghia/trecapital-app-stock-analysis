# Chapter 11 Phase 11A V82 — Formula / Boundary Explanation

Phase 11A is a source-lock phase, so it intentionally introduces **no new financial formulas**.

Chapter 11 later asks the analyst to examine deal price and financing, including multiples such as EV/EBIT, EV/EBITDA, free-cash-flow multiples, enterprise value to book value, and financing by cash, debt, equity, or combinations. It also discusses debt-coverage stress under revenue declines. These are source requirements for later evidence/data phases, not calculations implemented in V82.

The architecture rule is therefore:

- reuse canonical financial/company-data SSOT where those metrics already exist;
- do not create a duplicate deal-valuation, debt, cash-flow, or return engine inside Chapter 11;
- preserve the distinction between observed data and analyst conclusions;
- do not convert thresholds or examples in the book into an automatic acquisition-quality score;
- do not convert research completeness into an investment recommendation.

V82 does not calculate acquisition success, synergy realization, post-deal returns, intrinsic value, MOS, or a BUY/HOLD/SELL conclusion. It also does not modify the existing investment Research Gate.
