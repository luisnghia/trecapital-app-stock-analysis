# Chapter 11 Phase 11B V83 — Source-Locked Evidence Dimensions

## Source lock

- Michael Shearn — *The Investment Checklist*
- Chapter 11 — **Evaluating Mergers & Acquisitions**
- Q58 — printed page 305: **How does management make M&A decisions?**
- Q59 — printed page 310: **Have past acquisitions been successful?**
- Chapter range used for evidence dimensions: printed pages **305–322**.

## Scope

Phase 11B converts the source chapter into neutral evidence requirements only. It does not score M&A quality or decide whether a historical acquisition succeeded.

### Q58 — 8 dimensions

1. Decision process and rationale: merits, prospects, costs and risks.
2. Management motivation: economic/customer logic versus size, ego, excitement or empire building.
3. Core/customer-base fit versus unrelated expansion.
4. Revenue-synergy assumptions and customer behavior.
5. Cost-synergy assumptions and realizability.
6. Customer-overlap / synergy fit.
7. Roll-up economics and leverage risk.
8. Consistency of reasoning across smaller historical deals as evidence for future capital-allocation behavior.

### Q59 — 7 source-locked evaluation lenses

1. Fit with core competencies, including distribution and sales method.
2. Whether management intimately understands the target business before acquisition.
3. Customer retention after acquisition.
4. Employee/talent retention and culture preservation.
5. Price discipline, overpayment risk and willingness to walk away.
6. Price paid and post-deal economics, including multiples and book-value premium.
7. Financing method and risk tolerance: cash, debt, equity or a combination.

## Architecture boundary

The chapter names financial measures such as EBITDA, EBIT, FCF, book value, debt coverage and equity issuance. In V83 these are **SSOT dependency labels only**. Chapter 11 does not calculate or duplicate them. Later bridge phases must consume the existing canonical financial/data SSOT.

The following remain explicitly out of scope:

- automatic M&A score or weighted score;
- automatic successful/failed-acquisition conclusion;
- automatic synergy forecast;
- intrinsic-value engine;
- MOS change;
- Investment Research Gate change;
- BUY/HOLD/SELL or portfolio action;
- web research;
- database/store;
- Streamlit UI.

Missing evidence remains **Unknown** until an analyst reviews it.
