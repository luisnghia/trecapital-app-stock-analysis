# Formula / SSOT Explanation — Chapter 11 Phase 11B V83

Phase 11B intentionally adds **no new financial formula engine**. The book's quantitative references are represented as dependency labels so later phases can read the existing canonical financial/data SSOT.

## Source-referenced metrics

- Acquisition consideration: cash, debt and/or equity consideration.
- EV/EBIT, EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF: observed transaction multiples when canonical inputs are available.
- Premium to book value: observed acquisition price relative to canonical book value.
- Debt coverage and free cash flow: used to evaluate financing risk and resilience under weaker revenue scenarios.
- Equity issuance / shares outstanding: used to understand dilution when stock finances acquisitions.

## Explicitly not calculated in V83

V83 does **not** calculate acquisition IRR, synergy NPV, post-deal ROIC, EV/EBIT, EV/EBITDA, EV/FCF, debt-service capacity, book-value premium, dilution, or intrinsic value. It does not create a second copy of EBITDA, EBIT, FCF, book value, debt, cash, shares outstanding, or acquisition consideration.

## Research boundary

The seven Q59 lenses from the source are not weights and are not converted into a numerical M&A score. `Evidence found` only means that research material exists for a dimension; it does not mean favorable evidence. `Not found` is a research outcome, not a negative investment conclusion. `Unknown` remains the default when evidence has not been reviewed.

The analyst remains the owner of all qualitative conclusions.
