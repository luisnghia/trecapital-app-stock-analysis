## Phase 11A purpose

V82 establishes only the source contract and neutral analyst/research payload for Q58-Q59. The source chapter frames M&A analysis around understanding management's rationale and process, then evaluating historical acquisition outcomes. Detailed source-locked evidence dimensions are deferred to Phase 11B.

## Analyst ownership and Unknown-first behavior

Every question starts with Research Status `Unknown`, Confidence `Unknown`, and Analyst Assessment `Unknown`. Missing evidence remains a research gap. The module does not infer whether an acquisition process or historical acquisition record is good, bad, value-creating, or value-destroying.

## Explicit boundaries

Phase 11A adds no automatic M&A score, no weighted score, no synergy forecast, no acquisition-success model, no intrinsic-value engine, no MOS modification, no investment Research Gate modification, and no BUY/HOLD/SELL signal. It adds no web research, database/store, Streamlit UI, or financial data bridge. Later phases must reuse canonical financial/company-data SSOT rather than duplicating deal, debt, cash-flow, valuation, or return calculations.
