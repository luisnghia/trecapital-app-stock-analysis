# Appendix B — Phase A / V93 — Source Lock & Architecture Mapping

## Source lock

**Book:** Michael Shearn — *The Investment Checklist*  
**Appendix B:** **How to Interview the Management Team**  
**Print-page scope:** 331–334

Appendix B is implemented as a **management-interview research protocol**, not a scoring chapter and not a new investment decision engine.

## Source-locked sections

1. **Ask Open-Ended Questions**
2. **Be Aware of the Danger of Face-to-Face Assessments of Managers**

## Interview protocol

The V93 contract preserves the book's framing:

- ask one open-ended question at a time;
- listen before moving on;
- neutrally clarify, rephrase, or re-ask when an answer is incomplete or unclear;
- prefer evidence about what management actually did and why;
- do not treat hypothetical answers as evidence of future behavior.

## Management interview topic clusters

V93 maps the Appendix B examples into eight source-locked topic groups without converting them into scored criteria:

1. management style and management-team selection;
2. operational changes and inherited-versus-created practices;
3. major challenges over five or ten years;
4. career background, prior roles, and turning points;
5. mistakes and lessons learned;
6. best and worst parts of the job;
7. succession, strong managers, and future roles;
8. changes or improvements management would make.

## Face-to-face assessment caveats

Direct access is not treated as proof of management quality. V93 records possible false confidence, visual/body-language overweighting, snap presentation impressions, liking/similarity/common-interest/appearance bias, and accessibility/charisma bias as **review caveats only**.

The architecture keeps contextual checks separate: operating record, accomplishments, views of other informed sources, and evidence about how management thinks, operates, and measures the organization.

## Analyst ownership and AI boundary

AI remains a **Research Assistant**. It may later organize questions, notes, contextual evidence, and research gaps. The analyst owns interpretation, assessment, and conclusions.

V93 intentionally adds **no**:

- management score or weighted management score;
- CEO-quality classifier;
- credibility or personality score;
- BUY/HOLD/SELL output;
- intrinsic-value or Margin of Safety modification;
- Investment Research Gate modification;
- duplicate financial/company SSOT;
- automatic conclusion from face-to-face impressions.

## Phase-A architecture boundary

This phase is additive and deterministic only. Persistence, interview-session workspace, Streamlit UI, history/snapshots, and explicit linkage to existing DCA question research will be implemented in later Appendix B phases after this source contract is closed.
