# Appendix A — Phase A / V90 — Source Lock & Architecture Mapping

Source: Michael Shearn, *The Investment Checklist*, Appendix A — **Building a Human Intelligence Network**, print pages 323–330.

## Source-locked structure

1. **Evaluating Information Sources** — p.324
2. **How to Locate Human Sources** — p.324
3. **How to Contact Human Sources—and Get the Information You Want** — p.328
4. **Create a Database of Your Interviews for Future Reference** — p.329

Appendix A is implemented as a research-source workflow, not as a new investment-scoring chapter.

## Architecture mapping

- Public research remains upstream. Human-source work is used when material questions or assumptions remain unresolved.
- The model explicitly distinguishes **Primary** sources (first-hand knowledge) from **Secondary** sources (interpretation of other information). Secondary-source opinions are research leads, not accepted conclusions without supporting evidence.
- Human source records support customers, journalists, conference participants, industry insiders/associates, professors/deans, headhunters, management, employees, suppliers and competitors, matching the Appendix A source set.
- Contact workflow preserves the book's emphasis on researching the source before contact, explaining purpose/use, asking broad questions, requesting referrals, protecting identity, building trust and maintaining valuable contacts.
- Interview notes preserve the source's response separately from analyst commentary. When possible, statements can be marked Assumption, Theory, Fact, Question, Idea or Related point. Uncertainty must be recorded rather than silently rationalized.
- Source context/synopsis is stored separately from substantive statements so later analysts can understand the conversation context without rewriting what was said.

## Analyst ownership / AI boundary

AI is a **Research Assistant** only. It may organize source records, interview notes and research gaps. It must not decide whether a source is credible enough to change an investment conclusion and must not infer what a source 'really meant.' Analyst interpretation stays explicit and separate.

V90 intentionally adds no source-quality score, credibility score, weighted research score, BUY/HOLD/SELL logic, intrinsic-value/MOS change, Investment Research Gate change, web crawling/contact action, or duplicate financial/company SSOT.

## V90 scope

V90 establishes the source contract, data vocabulary, Unknown-first payload, deterministic normalization, tests, QA and dedicated CI. Persistence/UI/contact tooling is intentionally deferred to later Appendix A phases so the source lock can be validated before implementation expands.
