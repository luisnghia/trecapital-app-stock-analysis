# Chapter 9 Phase 9B — Source Contract V63

## Scope

Phase 9B deepens the source lock for Michael Shearn, *The Investment Checklist*, Chapter 9 — **Assessing the Quality of Management—Positive and Negative Traits** — while preserving Phase 9A's exact question range **Q48-Q52**.

This phase does **not** add a research agent, web crawling, UI, database persistence, financial-data bridge, management score, MOS change, Research Gate change, or BUY/HOLD/SELL signal.

## Source-derived contract

The contract distinguishes four source origins so that the app does not pretend every research cue is an exact checklist question:

- **Explicit prompt** — a question printed explicitly in the book.
- **Named subsection** — a named research subsection in Chapter 9.
- **Explicit trait list** — an enumerated list printed in the source.
- **Source paragraph** — an operational evidence dimension derived directly from an explicit explanatory paragraph in the source.

### Q48 — Does the CEO love the money or the business? (p. 256)

Six exact prompts under “How Do You Identify Passion?” are retained:

1. Is the business a career or just a job for the manager?
2. Would the CEO refuse to sell the business, no matter what the price?
3. Is the manager interested in money or motivated by money?
4. Does the manager focus on appearances instead of the business?
5. What type of philanthropic endeavors is the manager involved in?
6. Are the managers lifelong learners who focus on continuous improvement?

The evidence contract records career/industry history, sale-offer behavior, lifestyle/spending indicators, outside social commitments, philanthropy patterns, and continuous-improvement behavior. Lifestyle and philanthropy are explicitly treated as indicators rather than proof of ethics or motivation.

### Q49 — Can you identify a moment of integrity for the manager? (p. 264)

Phase 9B separates:

- consistency between words and actions;
- a documented integrity moment under stress, adversity, crisis, or an ethical situation;
- the explicit five-pattern adversity-response list on p. 267;
- long-term problem solving versus a quick short-term remedy.

The five source response patterns are preserved without converting them into a score. Absence of a documented integrity moment means the evidence remains **unknown**, not that the manager lacks integrity.

### Q50 — Are managers clear and consistent in communications and actions with stakeholders? (p. 268)

Eight dimensions are source-locked:

- sequential annual-report shareholder letters;
- historical conference-call transcripts/Q&A;
- communication under adversity;
- whether management only emphasizes good news;
- “Is the manager easy to listen to?”;
- “Do you learn from the manager?”;
- “Does the manager use corporate speak?”;
- “Does the manager use double speak?”.

The five exact shareholder-letter elements on p. 269 are retained: what is important, decision drivers, issues encountered, important health metrics, and how the CEO plans to resolve issues.

### Q51 — Does management think independently and remain unswayed by what others in the industry are doing? (p. 275)

Three source-paragraph dimensions are retained:

- resistance to copying competitors merely because peers are earning attractive profits;
- long-term focus despite pressure to maximize short-term profits;
- an independent operating plan rather than continually benchmarking against or copying competitors’ past success.

Peer behavior is context only; it is not itself a management-quality benchmark.

### Q52 — Is the CEO self-promoting? (p. 276)

Phase 9B preserves the exact six self-promoter traits on p. 277:

- Flamboyant.
- Have lots of charisma.
- Engage in aggressive salesmanship.
- Tend to command the center of attention.
- Take over discussions.
- Have an attitude that they are smarter than everybody else.

It also tracks Wall Street/sell-side conference frequency and purpose, financial-media/TV activity, stock-price focus, and the explicit financing-context exception. The book’s “more than two to four times per month” language is retained as contextual text rather than converted into an artificial numeric threshold. Promotion tied to genuine debt/equity/acquisition financing needs is preserved as an exception/context control.
