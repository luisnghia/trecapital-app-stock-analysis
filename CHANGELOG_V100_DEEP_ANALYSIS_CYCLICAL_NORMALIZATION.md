# V100 — Deep Company Analysis Cyclical Normalization

Base: `feature/deep-company-analysis-financial-semantics-v99` @ `f97c35609d7eec1c8418419379f96c4629f979cc`.

Adds a read-only 5–10 year cyclical normalization and historical comparability layer for revenue, gross margin, EBIT/operating margin, ROIC and canonical net income. Includes median, trimmed mean, P25/P75 scenario range, observed peak/trough, current TTM overlay, cycle-zone flags, explicit comparability-break surfacing, provenance and analyst/valuation guardrails.

No financial SSOT duplication, valuation/MOS change, weighted score, automatic BUY/HOLD/SELL or Research Gate change is introduced.
