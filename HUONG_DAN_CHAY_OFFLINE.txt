TREC CAPITAL - PHAN TICH CHUYEN SAU DOANH NGHIEP
=================================================

GOI LITE NAY DUOC DONG GOI DE CHAY LOCAL/OFFLINE TREN WINDOWS 64-BIT CO PYTHON 3.11.
Khong can Git, khong can clone repo. Theo yeu cau, goi Lite KHONG kem Python va KHONG kem offline_wheels de giam dung luong.

CACH CHAY NHANH
1. Giai nen file ZIP ra mot thu muc rieng.
2. Double-click: CHAY_TRECAPITAL_OFFLINE.bat
3. Neu Python 3.11 va cac thu vien Trecapital da co tren may, app mo ngay va co the nhap/luu/doc lai Chapter 1 offline.
4. Neu thieu thu vien, launcher se goi CAI_THU_VIEN_MOT_LAN.bat. Goi Lite khi do can Internet mot lan de pip install requirements.
5. Sau khi thu vien da cai, cac lan sau chi can double-click CHAY_TRECAPITAL_OFFLINE.bat.
6. Chon menu: Phan tich chuyen sau doanh nghiep.

YEU CAU
- Windows 64-bit.
- Python 3.11 64-bit da cai va lenh python co trong PATH.
- Thu vien trong requirements.txt da cai. May da dung cac ban Trecapital truoc thuong da co san.

DU LIEU CHUONG 1
- Luu local bang SQLite tai data_cache/deep_company_analysis_chapter1.db.
- Khong can OpenAI API, Supabase hay Internet de nhap, luu, mo lai, xem Opportunity Inventory va Review Queue.
- Nut "Cap nhat du lieu & signals" can Internet vi chu dong lay du lieu thi truong/BCTC moi tu pipeline Trecapital.
- Nut "Quet Review Queue tu du lieu cache" co the chay bang du lieu da luu local.

LUU Y
- Khong xoa thu muc data_cache neu muon giu lich su phan tich da nhap.
- Research Gate do analyst quyet dinh; app khong tu dong doi Gate.
- Event/CBTT tu WebEvidence chi la ung vien can analyst xac minh.
